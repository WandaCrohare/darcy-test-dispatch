DEPLOY MASTERS

A retro TCG with the team · 2 players, 1 keyboard

HOW TO PLAY
· Energy refills every turn: turn 1 gives you 1, turn 2 gives 2, and so on up to 10. Unspent energy is lost.
· Playing a card costs its yellow number. The first one becomes your active; the rest go to the bench (3 spots).
· Your active attacks as many times as your energy allows. Damage hits the enemy active; if they have no active, it hits them directly.
· Each player has 30 HP. Bring the other player's HP to 0 to win.
· Once per turn you can switch your active with a benched card, for free.
· A card can attack the same turn it's played — as long as you have the energy.
· Rumor says there's a legendary card hiding in one of the decks. Don't waste it.
· Player 2 can be a CPU opponent — flip the toggle before pressing PLAY.
